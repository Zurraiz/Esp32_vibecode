LEVEL 1.1: Your First Blink
Introduction
In this lesson, you will build your first working ESP32 program.
You will control a small LED using blocks and make it turn ON. This is the first time you will see how instructions you create in the system directly affect a physical (or simulated) output.
The goal of this lesson is not just to make the LED turn on, but to understand that:
Your blocks are instructions
The ESP32 executes those instructions
The output changes based on your instructions
By the end of this lesson, you will have created a complete working cycle of input instructions that produce visible behaviour.
