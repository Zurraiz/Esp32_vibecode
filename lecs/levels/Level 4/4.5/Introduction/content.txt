LEVEL 5.5: Pattern Systems (Complex LED Patterns)

## Introduction

In this lesson, you will learn how to create patterns using LEDs by combining loops, timing, and control logic.
 You will design sequences where LEDs turn ON and OFF in a specific order, creating visible patterns. This is the first time you will see how your program can generate structured and creative behavior.
The goal of this lesson is not just to blink LEDs, but to understand that:
Your blocks are instructions
The ESP32 executes those instructions
You can control the order of actions
You can repeat sequences to form patterns
By the end of this lesson, you will have created a system where multiple instructions work together to produce repeating LED patterns, helping you build more complex and visually interactive programs.
