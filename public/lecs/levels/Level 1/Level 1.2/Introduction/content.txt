LEVEL 1.2: Making Changes Visible (Using Delay)
Introduction
In the previous lesson, you controlled an LED by turning it ON and OFF using blocks.
However, you may have noticed something unusual:
Even though you gave instructions to turn the LED ON and then OFF, it was difficult to clearly see both actions.
[image: section_0.png]
This happens because the ESP32 executes instructions extremely fast.
In this lesson, you will learn how to slow down the execution so that changes become visible and easier to understand.
