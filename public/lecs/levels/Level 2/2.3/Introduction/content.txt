
## Level 2.3: Reading Input States


## Introduction

In this lesson, you will learn how the ESP32 reads and understands input signals from a button.
 You will detect whether a button is pressed or not and observe how this information is represented inside the program. This is the first time you will see how physical actions are converted into digital values.
[image: section_0.png]
The goal of this lesson is not just to read a button, but to understand that:
Your blocks are instructions
The ESP32 executes those instructions
The ESP32 reads electrical signals from inputs
Physical actions are converted into digital values (HIGH and LOW)
By the end of this lesson, you will have created a system where the ESP32 reads input states and represents them as values that can be used in your program.
