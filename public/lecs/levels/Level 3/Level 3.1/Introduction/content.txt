
## LEVEL 3.1: Reading Changing Values (Analog Input)


## 1. Introduction

In the previous levels, you worked with signals that had only two states:
ON or OFF
HIGH or LOW
However, not all inputs in real-world systems behave this way.
Many inputs change continuously instead of switching between two fixed states.
In this lesson, you will explore a new type of input where values are not limited to just two options. Instead, they can vary across a range.
[image: 1_0.png]
By the end of this lesson, you will understand how the ESP32 reads changing values and how these values behave over time.
