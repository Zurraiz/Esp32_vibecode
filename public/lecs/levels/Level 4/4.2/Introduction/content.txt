
## LEVEL 4.2: Conditional Repetition (While Loop Basics)


## Introduction

In this lesson, you will learn how to repeat actions based on a condition using a while loop.
 You will create programs where the ESP32 continues performing actions as long as a condition remains true. This is the first time you will see how repetition can depend on a condition instead of a fixed number.
[image: section_0.png]
The goal of this lesson is not just to repeat actions, but to understand that:
Your blocks are instructions
The ESP32 executes those instructions
The ESP32 can repeat actions based on a condition
The repetition stops automatically when the condition becomes false
By the end of this lesson, you will have created a system where actions continue only while a condition is true, giving you more control over program behavior.
