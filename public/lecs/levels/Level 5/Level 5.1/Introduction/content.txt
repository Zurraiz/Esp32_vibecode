
## LEVEL 6.1: Temperature and Humidity (DHT Sensor Basics)


## 1. Introduction

Until now, you have been working with simulated inputs like sliders and basic analog signals.
[image: 1_0.png]
In real systems, however, computers often read data from the physical world.
One of the most common types of real-world data is environmental data:
Temperature
Humidity
In this lesson, you will learn how the ESP32 reads data from a sensor that measures these conditions.
Instead of manually changing values, the system will now receive information from the environment.
