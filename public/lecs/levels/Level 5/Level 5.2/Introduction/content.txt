
## LEVEL 6.2: Motion Detection (PIR Sensor)


## 1. Introduction

In the previous lesson, your system was observing the environment by reading temperature and humidity.
In this lesson, the system will go one step further.
Instead of continuously measuring values, it will now detect events—specifically, movement.
A PIR sensor (Passive Infrared Sensor) allows the ESP32 to detect when a person or object moves in its field of view.
This introduces a new type of input:
Not continuous values
But event-based signals
