
## LEVEL 6.4: Sound Output (Buzzer Control)


## 1. Introduction

In the previous lessons, your system learned how to:
Detect motion using PIR sensors
Measure distance using ultrasonic sensors
Observe real-world conditions continuously
So far, the ESP32 has only been receiving information.
In this lesson, the system will take a new step:
 It will produce sound based on conditions.
[image: 1_0.png]
You will use a buzzer to generate sound signals that respond to sensor input.
This introduces a new idea:
The system can now communicate back to the physical world
